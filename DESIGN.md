## Overview
Aura Coaching is a high-fidelity design language for professional consultants, utilizing a warm palette and elegant, thin typography to convey stability and emotional intelligence.

## Colors
- Primary (Terracotta): #AF4B31
- Surface (Oatmeal): #F1EBE1
- Text: Primary, with 70% opacity variations for secondary information.

## Typography
- Headings: Poppins (300-600 weight).
- Body: Inter (300 weight) for clean readability.
- UI elements: Uppercase, tracked wide for professional elegance.

## Spacing
- Standard section padding is 6rem vertical.
- Grid gaps maintain consistent 2rem to 4rem intervals to promote breathing room.

## Layout
- Max-width: 72rem (6xl).
- Centralized alignment with asymmetric image/text sections for editorial feel.

## Elevation & Depth
- Low-depth shadow usage; focus is on border-based separation (1px).
- Backdrop-blur for floating UI elements to maintain context.

## Shapes
- Large rounded corners (lg: 16px) for major containers.
- Perfect circles for user portraits and icon triggers.

## Components
- Navigation: Fixed bottom/top floating bar with animated underline indicator.
- Buttons: Pill-shaped, transparent-to-solid state transitions.
- Forms: Minimalist approach, text-heavy contact sections.

## Motion
- Smooth scrolling enabled throughout.
- Hover effects for text reveal patterns and button state transitions.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do use high-quality, desaturated imagery.
- Do maintain large whitespace margins.
- Don't use heavy, solid black text; stick to the primary color palette.

## Accessibility
- High contrast ratios maintained against the #F1EBE1 background.
- Consistent focus states for keyboard navigation.
